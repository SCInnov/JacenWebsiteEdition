Restore Movement. Regain Independence.

The Rebo is a powered upper limb orthosis designed to support arm movement for individuals with muscle weakness or limited mobility.

Real-Time Intent Detection
Sensors detect subtle muscle signals to anticipate and assist your intended movements naturally.
Adaptive Assistive Support
Intelligent motor assistance adjusts in real-time to provide exactly the help you need.
Data-Driven Prognosis
Track progress and recovery metrics to inform clinical decisions and therapy planning.
